# Makeup Case RFQ Specification Checklist

Use this checklist before asking a supplier for a quotation or sample. Its purpose is to turn a broad request such as “professional makeup case” into a brief that can be quoted, sampled and compared consistently.

> **Scope note:** This is a buyer-planning template, not a product specification. Materials, MOQ, lead time, compliance documents and final configuration must be confirmed for the selected model, order quantity and destination market.

## 1. Define the intended use

Start with the work the case needs to support. State:

- **User and setting:** makeup artist, salon, beauty school, retailer, travel retail, gifting or another use.
- **Transport pattern:** fixed-location storage, occasional travel, daily mobile work or checked-luggage transport.
- **Contents:** approximate product types, including palettes, brushes, bottles, electrical tools or fragile accessories.
- **Access priority:** fast access during service, secure transport, display, separated storage or compact packing.
- **Target market:** country or region where the finished product will be sold.

This context helps prevent a quote for a visually similar case with an unsuitable structure or layout.

## 2. Specify the case format and dimensions

Record the details that affect layout and freight planning:

- Handheld, train case, trolley case, makeup bag, vanity case or modular workstation.
- Closed external dimensions in **L × W × H**.
- Required internal storage zones or usable compartment sizes.
- Desired tray, drawer, divider, pouch, brush-holder or mirror arrangement.
- Empty-weight limit, if handling comfort or shipping cost is important.
- Whether a telescopic handle, wheels, shoulder strap or side hook is required.

If an existing reference sample is available, provide clear photos of every side, the open layout and a measurement reference. Identify which features are essential and which are only visual preferences.

## 3. Describe construction as a system

A material name alone is not a complete specification. Separate the brief into these layers:

| Area | What to define |
|---|---|
| Core or shell | Example: panel-based structure, molded shell or fabric body; ask the supplier to identify the actual construction. |
| Surface finish | Texture, color, print, coating or exterior fabric. Confirm how it will be evaluated on the approved sample. |
| Frame and corners | Aluminum frame, reinforced edges, corner protectors or other structural components where applicable. |
| Interior | Lining, foam, dividers, elastic straps, tray finish and cleaning expectations. |
| Hardware | Latches, hinges, handles, rivets, zippers, wheels and pull rods; note the required function for each. |

Ask for the complete material stack to be written into the quotation or sample specification. This makes a later comparison more reliable than comparing marketing names alone.

## 4. Capture branding and visual requirements

List each brand element separately:

- Logo method: printed, embossed, metal plate, label, embroidery or another specified method.
- Artwork file format and color reference.
- Logo location, size and orientation.
- Exterior and interior color references.
- Packaging artwork, barcode, inserts, warnings or carton marks, if needed.

A logo concept should not be treated as approved production artwork. Confirm the artwork, placement and appearance on the pre-production sample.

## 5. Ask for a quotation you can compare

Request that the quotation identifies:

- The exact configuration being priced.
- The order quantity and currency.
- Included and excluded tooling, branding, packaging or freight items.
- Sample scope and sample-approval route.
- Available packaging options and carton information when known.
- Any dependency on order quantity, destination, material choice or customization.

Avoid treating an indicative online price or a different configuration as the final price for your brief.

## 6. Plan sample approval before bulk production

Before approving a sample, verify it against the agreed brief:

- External and internal dimensions.
- Layout, tray movement, drawer action and closure alignment.
- Handle, wheel, pull-rod, hinge, latch, zipper or strap operation where applicable.
- Material finish, stitching, edge treatment, rivets and visible workmanship.
- Branding artwork, color and position.
- Product fit using representative contents.
- Packaging, labels and carton marks when those are part of the order.

Photograph the approved sample and retain the signed specification, approved artwork and any agreed revisions together. These documents are the reference point for production communication and inspection.

## 7. Confirm market-specific requirements

If your product includes electrical components, batteries, mirrors, lighting, chemical materials or regulated packaging, identify the destination market early. Ask the supplier which documentation can be provided for the **specific configuration** and what additional testing or buyer-side verification may be required.

Do not assume that a document for another product, market or configuration applies to your order.
