# Trolley Makeup Cases

## 4221 Rolling Makeup Bag

4221 uses removable EVA dividers to organize its main compartment around the intended tool assortment.

- Standard divider set: 10 boards — five short, four medium and one long.
- Adjustment: the boards are held in slots and can be removed and repositioned only at compatible existing slot locations.
- Approximate usable main-compartment space: 38 × 35 × 10 cm. The space available within each section depends on the installed divider arrangement.
- Divider dimensions: short 11.5 × 9.8 × 1 cm; medium 23.2 × 9.8 × 1 cm; long 39 × 9.8 × 1 cm. The 9.8 cm measurement is the divider width.

The 39 cm long-board measurement includes the projecting ends that enter the slots. The 38 cm usable compartment span excludes the space occupied by the lining. These measurements follow the same direction but use different boundaries.

Check the selected layout with the intended contents before approving a sample.
