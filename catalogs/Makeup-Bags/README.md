# Makeup Bags

## BS-1 Folding Brush Bag

BS-1 combines zipped brush storage with a folding format for standing use on a tabletop.

- Estimated capacity: approximately 30–40 makeup brushes, depending on brush size and handle thickness. This is not a guaranteed capacity for every brush assortment.
- Brush layout: 14 elastic slots in two rows of seven; maximum total brush length is 23 cm.
- Protection: a soft PVC flap helps reduce direct contact between brush heads or with other items and provides limited splash protection. It is not a waterproof seal.
- Setup: unzip and open the bag, fold the upper section into position, then fasten the internal support structure to hold the bag upright.

Check fit and closure with the intended brush assortment.
